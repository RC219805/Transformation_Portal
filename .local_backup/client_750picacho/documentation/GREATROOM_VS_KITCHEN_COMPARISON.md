## Key Insights

### Great Room Advantages
1. **Superior baseline sharpness** - needs less intervention
2. **Better shadow detail** - excellent low-light preservation
3. **Higher edge strength** - exceptional architectural detail
4. **Well-centered exposure** - minimal global adjustments needed

### Kitchen Advantages
1. **Better highlight control** - less window clipping
2. **More uniform lighting** - easier to process
3. **Fewer extreme values** - more forgiving workflow

## Processing Strategy Differences

### Great Room Approach
- **Philosophy**: "Do no harm" - preserve existing excellence
- **Focus**: Selective highlight recovery, gentle material enhancement
- **Risk**: Over-processing will degrade quality
- **Key**: Use conservative parameters, avoid aggressive adjustments

### Kitchen Approach
- **Philosophy**: Moderate enhancement to lift quality
- **Focus**: Global exposure lift, clarity boost, material definition
- **Risk**: Under-processing may leave potential untapped
- **Key**: Balanced approach with room for adjustment

## Conclusion

Both renderings are **high quality**, but require **different processing philosophies**:

- **Great Room**: Already excellent → Conservative refinement
- **Kitchen**: Very good → Moderate enhancement

The Great Room's superior baseline means **less is more** - focus on subtle, selective adjustments rather than global transformations.
